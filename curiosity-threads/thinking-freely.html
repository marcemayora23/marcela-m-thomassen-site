Central Question

How do the beliefs we inherit shape the lives we build, and what happens when we begin to question them?

About

I grew up with Catholicism as the central framework for understanding the world, and eventually left the religion. Since then, I’ve become interested in the difficult process of rebuilding the beliefs and ideas that once gave my life meaning.

That experience made me curious about worldviews and belief in general (not just religious beliefs), but the ideas we inherit from our families, cultures and societies, and the beliefs we develop about ourselves, other people and how the world works.

I’m interested in how these beliefs influence the way we interpret reality and impact our actions, and how they can become either resources that empower us or structures that constrain us.
