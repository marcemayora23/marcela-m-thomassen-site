Essays Notes Great Work by Others
  • The Selfish Gene Book
    Richard Dawkins

    Introduced the idea of memes as units of cultural transmission — a framework that shaped how I think about inherited belief.

  • How to Do Great Work Essay
    Paul Graham

    A clear-eyed map of what it actually takes to do work that matters, it helped me enrich my view on ambition and the meaning of work.

  • The Art of Loving Book
    Erich Fromm

    The book contains some dated perspectives which I consider problematic (sexism & homophobia), however, Fromm's insights enriched my idea of the meaning of love, and helped me reframe some dated perspectives of my own.
